Green Salad

Description

A crisp, colorful green salad with fresh vegetables and a bright lemon-olive oil dressing. Great as a light lunch or a side to your favorite main dish.

Details

Prep time: 10 minutes | Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup feta cheese, crumbled
  • 1/4 cucumber, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Combine the mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, red onion, cucumber, and feta in a large bowl.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, salt, and pepper.
  3. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss gently until everything is coated.
  4. Serve immediately so the greens stay crisp.

Variations

  • Add sliced avocado, toasted nuts, or seeds for extra texture.
  • Swap feta for goat cheese or shaved Parmesan.
  • Mix in fresh herbs like basil, parsley, or mint for extra brightness.
