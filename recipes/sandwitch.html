Salmon Cheese Sandwich

Description

A tasty sandwich with flaky salmon, melted cheese, and fresh crisp vegetables. Ready in minutes and perfect for lunch or a quick dinner.

Details

Prep time: 10 minutes | Cook time: 5 minutes | Servings: 1

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 1 tablespoon butter, softened
  • 1 slice cheddar or Swiss cheese
  • 1/4 cup cooked salmon flakes
  • 1 teaspoon mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 2–3 thin slices of cucumber
  • 1 leaf lettuce or baby spinach
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Toast the bread slices until golden brown.
  2. Mix the cooked salmon with mayonnaise, lemon juice, and a pinch of black pepper.
  3. Spread butter on one side of each toast slice.
  4. Layer the salmon mixture on one slice, then top with cheese, cucumber, and lettuce.
  5. Close the sandwich and press gently, or enjoy open-faced.
  6. Optional: warm the sandwich briefly in a skillet until the cheese begins to melt.

Tips

  • Use leftover cooked salmon or canned salmon for a fast version.
  • Add a slice of tomato or a few capers for extra flavor.
  • For a creamier filling, stir in a little plain yogurt or cream cheese.
